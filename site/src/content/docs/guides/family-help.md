# Getting in — help for family members

This is the short guide for anyone who shares access to the home portal. No
technical background needed. If something here doesn't match what you're
seeing, contact your admin and they can send you a fresh sign-in link.

---

## How you sign in

When you open the app or the website, you'll see a big button that says
**Sign in with passkey**. Tap it. Your phone will ask you to confirm with
Face ID, Touch ID, or your device PIN — then you're in. That's it. No
username to remember, no password to type.

If your phone already has the passkey set up, this is the only step you'll
ever need.

---

## I got a new phone

Your passkey lives on your old phone (or in iCloud / Google's password
manager if you had sync turned on). On a fresh phone it might not be there
yet.

What to do:

1. On the sign-in page, tap the link at the bottom that says
   **New device or can't sign in?**
2. You'll land on a page that asks for your email. Enter it and tap
   **Send me a code**.
3. Open your email app. There will be a message from the home portal
   with a short numeric code. Enter that code on the next screen and tap
   **Verify code**.
4. You're in. While you're signed in, go to your account settings and add
   a passkey for this new phone so next time is one tap again.

If iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager is turned on, it may have
already copied your passkey to the new phone automatically. Give the
**Sign in with passkey** button a try first — it might just work.

---

## I lost my phone

If you have another device (laptop, tablet, old phone) that you've signed
in on before, start there. Tap Sign in with passkey and you should be
through.

If you don't have another device handy:

1. Borrow any phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Go to the sign-in page and tap **New device or can't sign in?**
3. Enter your email and use the code that arrives in your inbox.
4. Once you're in, you can add a passkey for whatever device you're now
   using.

If you can't get to your email either, reach out to your admin directly. They
can send you a fresh link that gets you straight in.

---

## I use both an iPhone and an Android

Passkeys on iPhone are stored in iCloud Keychain. Passkeys on Android are
stored in Google Password Manager. Those two systems don't talk to each
other automatically — so a passkey you set up on your iPhone won't appear
on your Android, and vice versa.

The easiest fix is to set up a passkey separately on each device you use:

1. Sign in on your iPhone (or Android) using your email code if needed.
2. Go to account settings and tap **Add passkey** or **Register this
   device**.
3. Repeat on the other device.

Alternatively, if you use a password manager like **Bitwarden** or
**1Password**, those apps can store passkeys and sync them across both
iPhone and Android. That's the smoothest path if you switch between both
regularly.

---

## I forgot my password / I never had a password

You don't need one. Passwords are a legacy path kept for a specific app
(Jellyfin / music) — not for everyday sign-in.

To get in, use your passkey (the **Sign in with passkey** button), or
use the email code path via **New device or can't sign in?**. Either one
gets you into your account without a password.

If something you're trying to sign in to asks for a username and password
(like the music app), message your admin and they'll sort out the credentials
for that specific app.

---

## Still stuck?

Text or message your admin. They can send a one-time link directly to your
phone that gets you in without needing a passkey or a code.
