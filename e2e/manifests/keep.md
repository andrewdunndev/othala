Keep is our **self-hosted backup** of the family password vault — a [Vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden) server on our own hardware. It mirrors a copy of our Bitwarden data as insurance.

**Use Bitwarden day-to-day** (the other tile) — that's the one we pay for and the source of truth. Keep is here only if we ever lose access to Bitwarden; it is not a second place to store passwords.
