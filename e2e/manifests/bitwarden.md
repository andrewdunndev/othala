This is the family's **primary** password vault — and the one we pay for, a [Bitwarden family plan](https://bitwarden.com/pricing/). Install it on every device and use it day-to-day.

We pay on purpose. Bitwarden has been remarkably generous in letting [Vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden) — the community server we self-host as **Keep** — exist alongside their product, and we run Bitwarden's own apps on every platform. Recurring revenue is how we say thanks and help keep that whole ecosystem healthy.

**Keep** (the other tile) runs Vaultwarden on our own hardware as a *periodic backup* of our Bitwarden data — insurance, not a replacement. Use Bitwarden day-to-day; Keep is there only if we ever need it.
